The business problem

Why this resource matters

When performance data lives in separate spreadsheets, software tools, and inboxes, leaders spend too much time gathering numbers and not enough time deciding what to do.

This workbook gives owners a starting point for reviewing revenue, job performance, cash flow, and operating priorities each week.

What it helps standardize

Built for daily use, not shelfware.

  • Revenue and weekly change
  • Open work and overdue items
  • Cash flow and receivables visibility
  • Priority issues and follow-up owners
  • Job costing and margin review
