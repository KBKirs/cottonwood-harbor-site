The business problem

Why this resource matters

Without a repeatable inspection process, vehicle condition depends on memory, habit, or whoever happens to notice a problem first.

This resource helps teams document vehicle readiness, flag issues, and keep field work moving with fewer avoidable delays.

What it helps standardize

Built for daily use, not shelfware.

  • Exterior and interior condition checks
  • Fluids, tires, lights, and safety equipment
  • Mileage and fuel tracking
  • Issue reporting and manager review
  • Clear pass, watch, and needs attention states
